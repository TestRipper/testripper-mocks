"Until death, all defeat is psychological."

Jo mara nahi, wo haara nahi.

Before death, nothing is final...keep working hard, despite the self-doubts and regrets...because times change, fates change...your future is still a story in the making...

Develop the capacity to study for long hours, away from every distraction. Be like the cat, solitary and a bit odd...don't gel into the mundane...

Struggle, for it's the supreme virtue. As Camus has said, to rebel is to exist.
